About Us

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena ‐ Johannesburg, established 2023.

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is an initiative by Jason Naidoo, an avid gamer who wanted to create a modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages could enjoy the latest gaming technology, compete in esports tournaments, and socialise with friends. He noticed that many gamers lacked access to high-performance gaming equipment and organised competitive events, so he established an arena that caters for casual players, competitive gamers, schools, and corporate events.

History

Founded in Johannesburg in 2023 by avid gamer Jason Naidoo.

Vision

A modern entertainment venue where gamers of all ages can play, compete and socialise.

Mission

Give individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs and businesses access to high-performance gaming technology and organised competitive events.

Goals

Host gaming events, esports tournaments, birthday parties, school outings and corporate team-building events.

Who We Serve

Our customers include individuals, families, schools, gaming clubs and businesses. Customers can select from a range of Gaming Packages and Individual Experiences, and may select more than one booking to receive a greater discount.
